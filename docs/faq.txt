FAQ

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Use the Forgot Password link on the sign-in page. A reset email should arrive within five minutes.

Q: Can I change my billing cycle?
A: Yes. Billing can switch eligible accounts between monthly and annual billing.

Q: Where can I see invoices?
A: Invoices are available under Account Settings, then Billing.

Q: How do I contact Sales?
A: Ask for pricing, demos, plan comparison, or enterprise options and the Sales team will help.

Q: Can support remember my previous issue?
A: Yes. The support system stores past interactions in local SQLite memory for later recall.
